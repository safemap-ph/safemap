# Safety Tips

## General Safety Tips

- Stay aware of your surroundings at all times.
- Avoid isolated or poorly lit areas at night.
- Keep your valuables hidden and secure.
- Use well-lit, busy routes when walking.
- Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, leave the area.
- Let someone know where you are going and when you expect to return.
- Keep your phone charged and carry emergency numbers.

## Online Safety Tips

- Do not share personal information with strangers online.
- Verify identities before trusting anyone online.
- Report suspicious online activity to the authorities.
- Never send money to people you have not met in person.
- Be careful with links and downloads from unknown sources.

## Safety Tips for Women and Children

- Call VAWC hotline 1388 if you or someone you know is experiencing violence or abuse.
- Visit the nearest WCPD desk at any police station for confidential assistance.
- Know your rights — the law protects you from violence, abuse, and exploitation.
- Keep a list of trusted contacts who can help in an emergency.

## What to Do in an Emergency

1. Call 911 immediately.
2. Note your exact location so you can tell the responders.
3. Stay calm and speak clearly.
4. Follow the instructions of emergency responders.
5. Do not confront attackers or suspects directly.
6. If you see something, say something — report it.

## Reporting Safety Incidents

You can report safety incidents anonymously through the SafeMap app:
1. Tap "Report Incident" on the main screen.
2. Fill in the details (what happened, when, where).
3. Submit the report — you will receive a reference code.
4. Your report will be reviewed by admin officers and may appear on the public map.
