## What to Do in Case of Fire

1. Stay calm.
2. Alert others nearby.
3. Call the emergency hotlines: 911, 117, or BFP at 160.
4. Evacuate the area if it is safe to do so.
5. Do not use elevators.
6. Close doors behind you to slow the fire.
7. If trapped, stay low to avoid smoke and signal for help.

## What to Do in a Natural Disaster

- Call CDRRMO Gensan at 552-3939 or 0943-461-4548.
- Call the national NDRRMC at 02-8911-5061.
- Follow evacuation orders from local authorities.
- Move to higher ground during floods.
