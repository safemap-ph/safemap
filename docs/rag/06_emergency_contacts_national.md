# Emergency Contacts — National Hotlines

## Immediate Emergency

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or 117 right now. These are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## National Emergency Numbers

- Emergency Hotline: 911 (24/7, all emergencies)
- PNP (Philippine National Police): 117 (24/7)
- PNP Text Line: 0917-847-5757
- PNP Website: pnp.gov.ph
- VAWC Hotline (Violence Against Women and Children): 1388 (24/7)
- WCPD (Women and Children Protection Center): 02-8532-5003 (24/7)
- DSWD (Department of Social Welfare and Development): 02-8931-8101 (24/7)
- DSWD SWAD Team NCR: 0932-529-8293 (24/7)
- BFP (Bureau of Fire Protection): 117 or 160 (24/7)
- Red Cross Philippines: 143 (24/7)
- NDRRMC (National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council): 02-8911-5061 (24/7)

## What Emergency to Call

- Crime in progress: Call PNP at 117 or 911.
- Fire: Call BFP at 117 or 160 or 911.
- Medical emergency: Call 911 or Red Cross at 143.
- Violence against women or children: Call VAWC at 1388.
- Natural disaster: Call NDRRMC at 02-8911-5061.

## Stay Safe Tips During an Emergency

- Move to a safe location if possible.
- Do not confront attackers or suspects.
- Note the location and any descriptions.
- Call for help immediately.
- Stay calm.
