## What to Do in a Medical Emergency

1. Call 911 or Red Cross at 143 immediately.
2. Do not move the injured person unless they are in danger.
3. Apply first aid if you are trained.
4. Stay with the person until help arrives.
5. Check the "Get Help Now" section on the SafeMap app for nearest hospitals.
