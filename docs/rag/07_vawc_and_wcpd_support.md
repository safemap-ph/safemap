# VAWC and WCPD Support

## What is VAWC?

VAWC stands for Violence Against Women and Children. The VAWC hotline (1388) is a 24/7 service that provides immediate assistance, legal advice, and safety guidance for victims of violence, abuse, and exploitation.

## What is WCPD?

WCPD stands for Women and Children Protection Desk. These are special desks located at police stations staffed by trained officers who handle cases involving women and children.

## How to Get Help

- Call the VAWC Hotline: 1388 (available 24/7, free and confidential).
- Call WCPD: 02-8532-5003 (24/7).
- Visit the nearest WCPD desk at any police station.
- Contact DSWD social workers for crisis intervention.
- Visit Women Crisis Centers for shelter and support.

## What to Expect When You Call

1. You will be connected to a trained female officer or social worker who specializes in VAWC cases.
2. Your conversation is strictly confidential and your identity will be protected.
3. They will provide immediate safety guidance, legal advice, or dispatch help if needed.

## Your Safety Matters

- All calls and reports are confidential.
- Your identity is protected at all times.
- You do not need to provide your real name to get help.
- DSWD can provide temporary shelter, financial assistance, and social case management.

## Find Nearest WCPD Desk

In the SafeMap app, go to the "Get Help Now" section and tap "Find Nearest WCPD Desk" to navigate to the closest Women and Children Protection Desk using your current location.
